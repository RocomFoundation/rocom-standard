Adopting ROCOM Changes Governance

Connecting robots, people, infrastructure and tasks in healthcare

ROCOM brings robot manufacturers, infrastructure providers, workforce platforms and task-system developers together around shared interfaces, governance and conformance requirements. The aim is coordinated healthcare operations, with clear responsibilities and reusable evidence that supports integration, procurement and local assurance.

Four control planes, one shared standard

The four blue layers of the O in our wordmark reflect these connected planes: distinct roles and expertise, working together through open interfaces.

Robot

Connect your robots to healthcare workflows through shared identity, capability and task interfaces, while retaining the specialist capabilities of your robots and fleet-management systems.

Infrastructure

Connect doors, lifts and shared resources through building management and access-control systems, with clear responsibilities, capacity limits and access permissions.

Workforce

Connect staffing and availability systems to coordinate human teams and robot capacity, respecting their distinct skills, responsibilities and operating constraints.

Task

Connect clinical and operational task systems to workforce and robot services through shared priorities, status updates and traceability.
